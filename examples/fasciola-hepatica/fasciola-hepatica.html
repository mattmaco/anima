Timings and biology are from WikiVet’s Fasciola hepatica entry: prepatent period 10 to 12 weeks; eggs need about 9 to 10 days at 22 to 26 °C to form miracidia; a miracidium must find a snail within about 3 hours; snail stages take about 6 to 7 weeks in favourable conditions; Galba truncatula is almost the only intermediate host in Europe. The average of 203 cercariae per miracidium comes from one 2013 experiment in G. truncatula at 26 °C (record); treat it as an estimate. The animation shows only a handful of cercariae.

The 26-second loop compresses real durations; the stage times are not a clock. For education only; this figure is not medical advice.
